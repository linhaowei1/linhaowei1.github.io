Two new benchmarks in the [Terminal-Bench](https://www.tbench.ai/) series are out: [Harbor-Index](https://harbor-index.org/blog/harbor-index), a challenging, diverse, and high-quality benchmark for evaluating frontier agents, which I co-lead; and [Frontier-Bench](https://frontierbench.ai/announcement) (formerly Terminal-Bench 3.0), for which I served as a task contributor and reviewer.
