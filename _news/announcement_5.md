Excited to share [Hyra](https://hy.tencent.com/research/hyra) (**H**un**y**uan **R**esearch **A**gent), an autonomous research agent I lead at Tencent Hunyuan. Hyra sets new state-of-the-art results on AI4AI, AI4Science, mathematics, engineering, and creative design (see [results](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hyra-results)). As one highlight, Hyra helped [settle an open problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27199) on the optimal exponent relating sumsets and difference sets in additive combinatorics (see the [announcement](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/2082655737541726636)).
